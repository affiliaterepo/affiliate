About the library

What VECORA is for

VECORA is an independent educational collection intended to help small online publishers make thoughtful decisions. It contains 200 practical guides arranged in ten fieldbooks.

Editorial approach

We distinguish examples from measured results. We do not invent testimonials, sales, conversions, sources or product guarantees. Pages emphasize transparent consent, accessible design and documentation of uncertainty.

The collection offers lessons and exercises; it does not provide personal financial, legal or tax advice. Consult current official provider policies for time-sensitive decisions.

Corrections

If a guide contains an error or an outdated statement, contact the publisher with the guide's address and the information that should be reviewed. Contact details appear on the contact page.

Commercial relationships

Some external resource links contain affiliate tracking. They are labeled near the link and explained in our disclosure policy. Read access and email subscriptions are optional.
